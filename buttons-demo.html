Samples: short labels, long labels, many options and a mixed set. The look is chosen with data-button-style="pill|list" on the embed, or per link with ?ethora-button-style= (the links above). The colour scheme is data-color-scheme="light|dark|system" or ?ethora-color-scheme=. Narrow the panel with ?ethora-width=340px to check small widths. Tapping a button sends it to the live demo room, so tap only if you mean to.
